A FEW HELPFUL DETAILS

Frequently asked questions.

Learn what to expect as you explore the Nafas demonstration.

What is Nafas?

Nafas is a mental wellness project with reflection tools and supportive resources. Some features are demonstrations rather than live services.

What support features are available?

You can explore a simulated AI Support chat, Daily Wellness mood check-ins and activities, a reflection field, and sample therapist session options.

How is personal information handled?

Each feature explains its behavior on its own page. The AI Support conversation and Daily Wellness check-in are not saved, and session requests are only prepared for the demo. Review each page's notice before sharing personal information.

How can I explore the available support options?

Use the service cards above to open the relevant Nafas page. You can also choose Get Started to visit the existing login page.
